The Strokes

Before Is This It.

NEW YORK CITY / LATE 1990s

Before Is This It, before the hype, before their name became synonymous with a new era of New York rock — there was simply a band trying to find its sound.

The Strokes emerged from New York City's music scene around Julian Casablancas, Nick Valensi, Albert Hammond Jr., Nikolai Fraiture and Fabrizio Moretti.

They weren't starting with arenas or worldwide attention. Their identity was built through rehearsals, small venues and the city's underground music scene.

The sound was stripped down. The guitars were sharp. The songs were direct. And the band's image felt almost deliberately indifferent.

Then the recordings began to travel beyond New York.

By 2001, Is This It had turned the band's early work into something much bigger. What began as a young New York band had suddenly become one of the defining stories of early-2000s rock.

Before the world knew The Strokes, they were just The Strokes.
